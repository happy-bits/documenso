# Documenso — Business Overview

Documenso follows an open-core business model: the core e-signature product is free, open source, and licensed under AGPLv3, while additional enterprise features (tracked in the `packages/ee` package) are reserved for paying customers. Revenue comes from a hosted SaaS offering at documenso.com and from an Enterprise plan aimed at larger organizations that need extra flexibility, support, and control. Community growth — GitHub stars, Discord, Product Hunt visibility — is a core part of the go-to-market strategy, since trust and adoption depend on visibility as much as marketing. The project recently paused accepting external pull requests in favor of a small group of trusted contributors, prioritizing code quality and maintainer velocity as the project scales. Openness itself is treated as a competitive differentiator against closed incumbents like DocuSign.

## FAQ

**AGPLv3?**
AGPLv3 is a strong copyleft open-source license. It requires that anyone who modifies the software and runs it as a network service must also make their modified source code available to users of that service, closing the loophole regular GPL has for hosted/SaaS use.

**Can you use the full product without paying?**
You can self-host and use the core open-source product for free under AGPLv3. Certain enterprise-only features and support are reserved for paying customers via the hosted plan or Enterprise plan.
